# Pass-2 training datasets

Generated by `scripts/build_catalog.py` from `plan/selection.jsonl`, the dataset registry and `catalog/notes*.yaml`. Machine-readable version: [`datasets.yaml`](datasets.yaml); open issues per dataset: [`FLAGS.md`](FLAGS.md). Do not edit by hand.

**420 datasets**, about **872,309 planned training images**. Licence groups: permissive 411, nc 9. Splits: train 366, val 54. Pilot validation: validated 384, download manual 22, no images prepared 9, download too_big 3, download error 2.

Columns: *Samples* is the size of the full dataset (images, plus volumes or videos where those are the unit); *Planned* is how many images we sample from it for training. *nc* marks the non-commercial layer (off by default: `include_nc`).
